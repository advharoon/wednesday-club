About this Lecture

Welcome to the inaugural lecture of the Wednesday Club Criminal Law Lecture Series. This portal contains the complete collection of research notes, reference books, judgments and supporting materials discussed during today's lecture. Participants are encouraged to read the research notes first, followed by the recommended books and case law compilations. This resource library will continue to grow with every lecture in the series.
