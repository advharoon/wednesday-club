About the Session

The Wednesday Club presents a session on The Doctrine of Legitimate Expectation: Estopped or Entitled?. This portal brings together the research notes, articles, books and case law collected for the academic discussion.

The materials are provided as a convenient reading and reference library for participants and members of the legal community.
