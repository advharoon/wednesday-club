Civil Law Lecture Series

Pleadings in Civil Suits: Principles, Practice, and the Ouster of Civil Court Jurisdiction

Inaugural Lecture by Justice Easwaran S., Judge, High Court of Kerala.

Date 07 October 2026
Time 4:40 PM
Venue KHCAA Hall
Inaugural Lecture · Civil Law Lecture Series

About the Session

The inaugural session of the Wednesday Club's Civil Law Lecture Series focuses on pleadings in civil suits, their principles and practical application, together with the important question of exclusion of civil court jurisdiction by special statutes.

Lecture

Pleadings in Civil Suits: Principles, Practice, and the Ouster of Civil Court Jurisdiction

Justice Easwaran S.
Judge, High Court of Kerala
